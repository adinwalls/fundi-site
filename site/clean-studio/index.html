About Fundi Maintenance

A trusted hand for your home

Fundi Maintenance is a home maintenance service based in George, in the Western Cape. We serve George and the surrounding areas, painting, building and maintaining both private homes and commercial buildings.

Most people wait for something to break before they renovate. We think the real magic is renovating to upgrade the way you live, not only to fix problems. Your space should work for the way you live and work, and make you smile when you walk through the door.

Services

What we do

From a single room to a full build, for private homes and commercial properties in George and surrounds.

Painting

Interior & exterior painting

Walls, ceilings, trims, doors and facades, with the preparation that makes a finish last through coastal weather.

  • Crack filling and surface prep
  • Interior walls and ceilings
  • Exterior walls, fascias and gutters
  • Wood and metal finishes
Waterproofing

Roofs, walls & wet areas

Stop leaks and damp at the source, before they turn into bigger repairs.

  • Flat and pitched roofs
  • Damp and rising-damp treatment
  • Balconies and parapet walls
  • Bathrooms and showers
Building

Building & renovations

Upgrades that change how your space works, from a kitchen refresh to an extension.

  • Kitchen and bathroom renovations
  • Additions and alterations
  • Plastering, tiling and ceilings
  • Paving and boundary walls
Maintenance

General maintenance & repairs

The list of small jobs you've been putting off, handled properly in one visit.

  • Doors, windows and handles
  • Minor plumbing and fittings
  • Rental turnovers
  • Scheduled upkeep plans
Commercial

Commercial & private properties

Offices, shops, complexes and rentals, with work planned around tenants and trading hours.

  • Body corporates and complexes
  • Offices and retail
  • Property managers and landlords
